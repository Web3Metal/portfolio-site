# Portfolio project instructions

## Purpose and positioning

This is primarily a professional portfolio and job-search asset. The central positioning is content strategist and creative producer, with growth, community, and systems experience supporting that identity. The site may also support consulting or project-based opportunities through a clearly separated path, but lead generation must not dominate the primary experience. Do not reposition Shawn primarily as a growth operator, freelancer, or artist.

## Content and factual accuracy

- Preserve approved copy unless the task requires a change. Do not rewrite unrelated sections.
- Never invent achievements, responsibilities, clients, metrics, dates, or outcomes.
- Distinguish work Shawn created, hosted, edited, directed, coordinated, managed, or otherwise contributed to. Ask Shawn when attribution or a claim is ambiguous.
- Before editing content, use the **Canonical content sources** map in `README.md`. Markdown records under `content/` are not automatically the source rendered by the app. Do not resolve conflicting copy or facts by guessing; preserve the discrepancy and ask when needed.

## Scope and assets

- Keep a contained request contained; do not redesign unrelated sections or restructure content without approval.
- Preserve existing assets. Do not delete, replace, overwrite, or publish media-library/reference files without an explicit scoped request. Prefer adding a separately named approved derivative over destructive changes.
- Use real portfolio artifacts when available and approved; do not substitute generic representations for real work without a reason.
- Verify responsive behavior at desktop and mobile sizes for visual changes.

## Review and publishing

- For small, reversible changes: implement, run relevant checks, inspect affected routes, and report what remains unverified.
- For major redesigns, positioning/brand changes, architectural changes, large restructures, or removal of existing content: propose the direction and impact first, then wait for approval.
- Ambiguous factual content requires Shawn's clarification rather than inference.
- Local preview approval is not production approval. Do not commit, push, deploy, or publish unless the request explicitly authorizes that action. Confirm the target branch and deployment configuration before a requested release.
