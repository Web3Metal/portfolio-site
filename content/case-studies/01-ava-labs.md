title: Ava Labs — Developer Engagement Manager
dates: Oct 2024 to Oct 2025
company_context: Ava Labs is the team behind Avalanche, creating high performance blockchain infrastructure, developer tools, and programs to support app and chain builders.

role_overview: Part of a small engagement team driving developer adoption of Avalanche. Managed developer relationships from first interaction through qualified interest, program participation, and handoff to deeper technical support.

challenge: |
  Avalanche wanted to increase the number of developers building long term projects in its ecosystem. The challenge wasn't simply generating awareness. It was identifying promising developers, understanding where they were in their journey, and connecting them with the right programs, events, and internal teams to help them continue building.

my_role: |
  I managed developer engagement through Sprinklr, identifying promising builders, nurturing ongoing conversations, segmenting audiences by persona, improving onboarding workflows, coordinating handoffs to Developer Relations, and collaborating across teams to strengthen the developer journey from first interaction to active builder.

the_system: |
  Rather than treating every interaction the same, I helped build an onboarding system that matched developers with resources based on their experience, interests, and stage in the builder journey. We identified underserved personas, improved engagement workflows, connected previously separate programs, and handed qualified developers to Developer Relations when they were ready for deeper technical support.

scope:
* Led day to day developer outreach on X and consistently ranked among the team's strongest performers for click through rate and lead discovery
* Used Sprinklr search and native X tools to identify, qualify, and nurture prospective builders
* Partnered with Sprinklr specialists to improve lead visibility and funnel tracking
* Built and deployed UTM tagged links to measure clicks in channels Sprinklr could not track directly
* Qualified leads and routed them into Avalanche programs including Elevate, Builders Hub, Hackathons, and Team1
* Held video calls with prospects, referred projects to Business Development when appropriate, and connected builders with ecosystem partners
* Helped identify underserved developer personas and improve the pathways connecting early interest to deeper program participation

results:
* Engaged more than 9,000 developers
* Drove more than 6,900 visits to Avalanche programs, resources, and events
* Managed more than 4,400 initial interactions, 3,500 continued conversations, and 600 follow ups
* Produced roughly 10,000 outreach posts, including about 1,700 linked posts that generated approximately 2,100 clicks
* Ranked among the team's strongest performers for click through rate and lead discovery
* Helped double program conversions from Q1 to Q2
* Contributed to a 29 percent improvement in clicks per message
* Supported a 229 percent month over month increase in Elevate conversions

assets:
* Rebuilt developer journey diagram
* Anonymized engagement funnel
* Selected public facing campaign examples
* Simplified before and after onboarding workflow

lessons: |
  Developer engagement works best when it is treated as a long term relationship rather than a sequence of isolated campaigns. The strongest results came from understanding each developer's stage, giving them a relevant next step, and maintaining continuity until they were ready for deeper technical support.
