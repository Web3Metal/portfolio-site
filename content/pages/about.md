# About

I’m a growth and community strategist, writer, producer, and creative technologist working at the intersection of storytelling, emerging technology, and online culture.

My work usually begins with something promising but incomplete: a product people do not yet understand, a community without a clear rhythm, a creator ecosystem missing its connective tissue, or an idea that needs a system around it before it can grow.

I have built developer engagement workflows for Avalanche, created recurring content systems for early-stage media and gaming companies, helped grow Cyber Metal Radio into an active creator community, and founded Web3 Metal as a platform for artists exploring heavy music, Web3, and AI.

My background is in journalism and political science, which taught me to ask better questions, find the real story beneath the obvious one, and translate complicated ideas into language people can actually use. That same instinct now shapes how I approach growth, community, content, and product experiments.

I am especially interested in creative technology: AI music, interactive media, new creator tools, and the strange new forms that emerge when artists get access to systems that previously belonged only to studios or large companies.

The thread connecting all of it is simple: I find the missing structure behind an idea and build what helps it become understandable, repeatable, and real.

## What I Work On

- Growth and community strategy
- Creator and developer ecosystems
- Content systems and editorial platforms
- Video, podcast, and social production
- AI music and creative technology experiments
- Early-stage products that are still figuring out what they are
