# Creative Lab

The Creative Lab is where I test ideas that do not fit neatly into a case study: AI music systems, interactive worlds, performance tools, narrative experiments, and early product concepts.

Some projects begin as working prototypes. Others are research questions wearing a trench coat. The goal is to explore what new creative tools make possible, then identify which ideas deserve to become real products, experiences, or platforms.

## Current Experiments

### AI Band Simulator

A narrative rhythm game built around music creation, where players write songs with AI bandmates, get signed, perform live, and shape the story through the music they create.

### Explorable Album Universe

An interactive world built around an artist’s music, where individual songs become locations, characters, stories, and playable experiences.

### Text-to-MIDI Workbench

An experiment in translating language, narrative structure, and emotional tone into editable MIDI patterns for further composition inside a DAW.

### Live AI Performance Systems

Tools and performance concepts that allow artists to control, reshape, or collaborate with generative music systems during a live show rather than simply playing finished AI tracks.

## What I’m Exploring

- AI-assisted music composition
- Interactive albums and narrative games
- Creator tools and artist-facing platforms
- Live performance interfaces
- Procedural storytelling and worldbuilding
- New ways for audiences to participate in creative work

## The Working Method

Each project starts with a question, becomes a rough system, and gets tested through prototypes, creative use, and real feedback. The useful ideas move forward. The rest become compost for the next strange machine.
